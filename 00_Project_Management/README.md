# 01_Project_Management

## Purpose
Central hub for planning, tracking, and coordinating the ALCSM thesis project across all tasks — from Task 1 through final submission and defense.

## What Belongs Here
- Thesis proposal and committee-approved scope documents
- Task/milestone timelines and Gantt charts
- Meeting minutes (advisor, committee, collaborators)
- Progress reports and status updates
- Risk logs and open-issue trackers
- Correspondence logs (email/meeting summaries relevant to project decisions)

## Naming Conventions
- Planning documents: `ALCSM_PM_<Description>_v<Major>.<Minor>.<ext>`
  - e.g., `ALCSM_PM_Timeline_v1.2.xlsx`
- Meeting notes (dated, one per event, not versioned): `ALCSM_PM_MeetingNotes_<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`

## Versioning Rules
- Planning documents follow standard MAJOR.MINOR versioning: MAJOR for scope/milestone changes approved by advisor/committee, MINOR for routine updates.
- Meeting notes are append-only records identified by date — never overwrite a past meeting's notes.
- Superseded planning documents (e.g., an old timeline replaced by a new one) move to `17_Archive/01_Project_Management/` rather than being deleted.
