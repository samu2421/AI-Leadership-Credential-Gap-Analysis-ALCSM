# 09_Review_Logs

## Purpose
The revision and consistency-review history of the governance package itself — distinct from `07_Institutional_Correspondence/` (external communication) and from `DECISION_LOG.md` (project-wide decisions of every kind). This folder tracks internal reviews of the ethics/GDPR documentation set specifically: what was checked, against what, and what (if anything) needed reconciling.

## What Belongs Here
- Governance/cross-reference consistency reviews of the `04_Ethics/` document set
- Records of conditions raised by a reviewer (internal or committee) and how/when each was closed
- Notes on assumptions or placeholders used in a given drafting pass, and their resolution once real values are supplied

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_<ReviewType>_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`
  - e.g. `ALCSM_Ethics_GovernanceConsistencyReview_v0.1-DRAFT.md`

## Versioning Rules
- Each review is its own dated/versioned document; a later review does not overwrite an earlier one — it references what changed since.
- Placeholder-resolution notes stay attached to the review that first flagged them, even after the placeholder is filled in elsewhere, so the history of "this used to be a placeholder" is not lost.
