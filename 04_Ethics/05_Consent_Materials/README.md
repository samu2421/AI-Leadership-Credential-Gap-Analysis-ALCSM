# 05_Consent_Materials

## Purpose
Consent-related materials for the expert-validation survey strand — the one part of this research where consent (Art 6(1)(a)), not legitimate interests, is the legal basis. Not used for the LinkedIn profile-audit strand, which does not seek consent from profile subjects (see `03_LIA/` for that strand's basis).

## What Belongs Here
- Survey participant information sheet
- Survey informed consent form
- Any translated versions, if the survey is offered in more than one language

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_SurveyInfoSheet_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`
- `ALCSM_Ethics_SurveyConsentForm_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`

## Versioning Rules
- The version and date shown to respondents must match the version referenced in the consent form's own text ("I have read... version X, dated Y") — keep the two documents' version numbers synchronised.
- Once survey responses exist against a given version, that version is frozen; a wording change ships as a new version, not an edit to the one respondents already saw.
