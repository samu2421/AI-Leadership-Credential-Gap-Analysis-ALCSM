# 03_LIA

## Purpose
The Legitimate Interests Assessment supporting the Article 6(1)(f) legal basis used for the LinkedIn profile-audit strand of this research (the survey strand relies on consent instead — see `05_Consent_Materials/`).

## What Belongs Here
- The LIA document itself: purpose test, necessity test, balancing test
- Any supervisor/DPO review notes on the balancing test specifically
- Records of how the LIA's conclusions are reflected in the privacy notice and DPIA (cross-references, not duplicated content)

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_LIA_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`

## Versioning Rules
- Revised whenever the processing's purpose, scope, or scale changes materially (e.g. a change to sample size or data fields collected) — never edited silently in place once reviewed.
- A DPO-endorsed LIA is immutable once endorsed; a later revision is a new version referencing it.
