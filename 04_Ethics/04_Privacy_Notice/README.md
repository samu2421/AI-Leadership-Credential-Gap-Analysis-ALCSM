# 04_Privacy_Notice

## Purpose
The public Article 14 privacy notice for data subjects whose profiles are collected without being contacted directly — the "make the information publicly available" transparency safeguard this project relies on instead of (infeasible) individual notification.

## What Belongs Here
- The privacy notice draft and its published version
- The record of where/when it was published (URL, date) once live
- Any revision history if retention periods, contacts, or scope change

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_PrivacyNotice_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`

## Versioning Rules
- The published version is immutable once live; a substantive change (new data use, new retention period) requires a new version, publicly dated, not a silent edit.
- Prior published versions are retained so a data subject who read an earlier notice can always be shown what it said at the time.
