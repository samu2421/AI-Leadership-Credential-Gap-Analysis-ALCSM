# 07_Institutional_Correspondence

## Purpose
The dated record of communication with the ethics committee, the Data Protection Officer, and the supervisor on governance matters — the trail that shows conditions were raised and how they were resolved.

## What Belongs Here
- Dated summaries of meetings and calls (not necessarily full transcripts)
- Email correspondence relevant to a governance decision (forwarded/exported, not screenshots)
- Committee queries and the responses sent back
- DPO guidance and clarifications

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_Correspondence_<YYYY-MM-DD>_<ShortDescription>.<ext>` — dated, not versioned (each entry is a point-in-time record).

## Versioning Rules
- Append-only by date; a correspondence record is never edited after the fact — a follow-up or correction is a new dated entry, not a rewrite of the original.
- Nothing here is deleted, including correspondence that turned out to be a dead end or a rejected proposal — it is part of the honest audit trail.
