# 01_Ethics_Approval

## Purpose
The formal ethics-committee application for this project and its supporting protocol annexes — the documents a reviewer needs to decide whether to approve real-data collection.

## What Belongs Here
- The ethics application draft and its submitted version
- The LinkedIn profile collection protocol (submitted as a supporting annex to the application)
- Committee decision letters and approval references, once received
- Any conditions attached to approval and how they were satisfied

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_<Description>_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`
  - e.g. `ALCSM_Ethics_ApplicationDraft_v0.1-DRAFT.md`, `ALCSM_Ethics_ApplicationDraft_v1.0-SUBMITTED.md`
  - The submission/approval *date* itself is recorded inside the document's own metadata block (see `Document Header` fields), not in the filename.

## Versioning Rules
- A submitted version is immutable once submitted — never edit it in place; a revision requested by the committee becomes a new minor/major version referencing the original.
- An approved version is immutable once approved. Amendments reference the amendment date and the approval they modify.
- Nothing here is deleted; the full submission history is retained, including any rejected or superseded drafts.
