# 06_GDPR_Documentation

## Purpose
The general GDPR compliance documentation that isn't specific to the DPIA or the LIA individually: the Data Management Plan, the legal-basis/Article 14 position memo, and a consolidated compliance checklist.

## What Belongs Here
- Data Management Plan (DMP)
- GDPR compliance checklist
- Legal-basis summary memo (which basis applies to which data strand, and why)
- Re-identification-mapping decision record (also cross-referenced from the DMP)

## Naming Conventions
- `ALCSM_Ethics_DataManagementPlan_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`
- `ALCSM_Ethics_GDPRComplianceChecklist_v<Major>.<Minor>[-STATUS].<ext>`

## Versioning Rules
- The DMP is a living document during data collection (retention/destruction dates, storage details may be refined) but every version is retained, dated, and never silently overwritten.
- The compliance checklist is re-run and re-dated at each major gate (before P3 collection, before P6 main collection) rather than treated as a one-time artefact.
