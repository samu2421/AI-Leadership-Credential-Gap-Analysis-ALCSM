# 11_Thesis_Writing

## Purpose
The thesis manuscript itself, from early chapter drafts through the final submitted document.

## What Belongs Here
- Individual chapter drafts
- Full-manuscript compilations
- Advisor/committee feedback and track-changes copies
- Defense presentation slides

## Naming Conventions
- Chapters: `ALCSM_Thesis_Ch<N>_<ChapterName>_v<Major>.<Minor>.<ext>`
  - e.g., `ALCSM_Thesis_Ch3_Methodology_v2.1.docx`
- Full draft: `ALCSM_Thesis_FullDraft_v<Major>.<Minor>.<ext>`

## Versioning Rules
- Never overwrite a version that has been sent for advisor/committee review — each review round is a new MINOR version.
- The final submitted version is locked as the terminal MAJOR version, explicitly marked, e.g., `ALCSM_Thesis_FullDraft_v5.0_SUBMITTED.pdf`.
- All prior review-round versions are retained permanently, moved to `17_Archive/11_Thesis_Writing/` once superseded.
